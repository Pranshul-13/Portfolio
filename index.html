01 About

Engineering past the demo

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, carrying a 3.82 GPA with four Dean's List semesters. My interest is narrower than "AI" in general — I care about the engineering that makes a model useful once it leaves the training loop: evaluation you can trust, latency you can measure, and interfaces people actually use.

At the People and Robots Laboratory I build the LLM pipeline and full-stack platform behind an approved 50-user language-learning study, including responsible-AI checks and an offline test suite that runs full regressions in under a minute at zero API cost. Separately I founded QuickLearn, an adaptive SAT prep platform with a real-time knowledge-tracing service and a leakage-safe evaluation pipeline.

Before that I was an Application Engineer Intern at Advaiya Solutions, working on a vehicle claim-assessment prototype and cutting agentic voice-assistant latency by 58%.

05 Before Madison

Background

I grew up in Hong Kong and finished secondary school there in 2024 with an IB diploma score of 42/45, then moved to Wisconsin. Before university my technical work was robotics — designing, building, and programming land and underwater robots, which took a national first prize at the World Robotics Olympiad in 2023. I taught myself Python in early 2023, starting with the usual text-based games and a URL-to-QR-code tool.

My first piece of independent research was the IB Extended Essay in Economics — a 4,000-word investigation carried out over roughly a year, with a self-chosen research question, primary and secondary data collection, and a defended conclusion. It's the closest thing I did before university to the research work I do now at the People and Robots Laboratory: pick a question, decide what evidence would actually answer it, then go and get it.

Outside the lab I ran a small home-tutoring service from 2017, teaching 30+ children aged 8–16 including students with additional learning needs. That's a large part of why adaptive learning is the problem I keep returning to — QuickLearn is the same question with better tooling: what should this particular student practise next?

I volunteered for two years with Fredie Hung Ethnic Minority Services, distributing 2,000+ masks, sanitisers, and supplies to over 500 low-income residents during the pandemic, and ran cultural presentations for local community centres. I also led collection teams for HandsOn Hong Kong and Breadline. A reference letter from the organisation's director is available on request.
